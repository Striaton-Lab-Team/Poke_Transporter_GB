0x4F (non-KOR) and 0x5F (KOR) place the current character at 0xC4E1.

Each use of 0x4E (non-KOR) and 0x59 (kor) starts writing 40 bytes after the start of the current line.

The location of the stack smash is not always constant:
	- For Gold/Silver (non-KOR), we aim to overwrite 0xDFCB and 0xDFCC
	- For Crystal, we aim to overwrite 0xE0BB and 0xE0BC
	- For Gold/Silver (KOR), we aim to overwrite 0xCFCB and 0x CDCC
